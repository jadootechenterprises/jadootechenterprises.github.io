How We Work

A clear five-step process from first conversation to deployed product.

  1. 01

    Discover

    We listen to your problem, ask the right questions and map out exactly what needs to be built.

  2. 02

    Plan

    We choose the right technology and create a clear plan before writing a single line of code.

  3. 03

    Build

    We develop and test iteratively. You see progress throughout, not just a finished product at the end.

  4. 04

    Test

    You run it in your real environment. We fix anything that does not work as expected.

  5. 05

    Launch

    We deploy, document and stay available for questions, tweaks and future additions.
